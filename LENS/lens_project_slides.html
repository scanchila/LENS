The paid artifact is not a list of ideas. It is an evidence-backed opportunity brief with assumptions, contradictions, and next validation steps.

Formal Object

The core unit is a candidate problem.

A candidate is a hypothesis that a specific actor experiences a costly gap between a current state and a desired state, and that the gap may be tractable, neglected, and worth validating now.

Source document, post, paper, transcript, dossier
Trace cited span or extracted datum
Claim atomic proposition with qualifiers
Signal pattern across traces
Candidate actor, gap, mechanism, assumptions
Brief decision artifact with kill criteria
c = (actor, current_state, desired_state, gap, proposed_mechanism, evidence_set, assumptions, validation_path)
Theoretical Framework

The theory is deeper than a prioritization checklist.

Decision foundations

  • Multi-attribute utility theory makes tradeoffs explicit.
  • Value-focused thinking starts with the user's mandate.
  • Value of information asks which validation action can change the decision.
  • Exploration/exploitation protects strange but grounded candidates from premature optimization.

Opportunity and evidence foundations

  • Entrepreneurial opportunity theory keeps actor fit central.
  • Creativity research separates novelty from usefulness and feasibility.
  • Argumentation theory maps claims to grounds, warrants, qualifiers, and rebuttals.
  • Wicked-problem theory keeps assumptions visible.

Operational adapters include ITN/SNT, ODI, GRADE, CHNRI, TRL, RICE, and Heilmeier, but they do not define the candidate.

Calibration

Weights begin explicit, then learn carefully.

Manual by default

w_effective = normalize(w_user + lambda * w_learned)

lambda starts at zero. The organization sees its stated mandate before any learned adjustment.

Staged learning targets

Stage 1predict human verdicts
Stage 2predict validation outcomes
Stage 3predict downstream portfolio outcomes

Inspectable

Show user weight, learned adjustment, effective weight, and evidence.

Versioned

Every calibration update must be reproducible from historical decisions.

Constrained

Sparse data recommends changes; it does not silently override a mandate.

One database, three workloads

  • Relational: sessions, runs, transcripts, scores, calibration, lifecycle events.
  • Vector: semantic retrieval over uploaded and curated documents.
  • Graph: claim to source, candidate to evidence, principle to analogy.
  • Object storage: MinIO keeps raw uploads out of application tables.

Evaluation

YC RFS prediction gives the system a falsifiable benchmark.

Method

  • Use only pre-cutoff public and private source material.
  • Generate RFS-style candidate areas with provenance.
  • Reveal held-out YC RFS list after prediction.
  • Score direct matches, adjacent matches, misses, excess, and provenance quality.
